Gujarat has among the highest rooftop solar adoption in India, and Ahmedabad sits at the centre of that story. A combination of strong state policy, high solar irradiance and proactive distribution companies has made rooftop solar mainstream for homes and small businesses across the state. This guide explains why Gujarat leads, and covers 2026 costs, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy and net metering in Ahmedabad.

## Why Gujarat leads India in rooftop solar

Gujarat's leadership is not an accident. Several factors reinforce each other:

- **High solar irradiance.** Gujarat receives some of the strongest, most consistent sunlight in the country, so systems generate reliably at roughly 4 units per day per kW.
- **Strong state policy.** Gujarat's solar policies and DISCOM processes have long been among the most supportive and streamlined in India.
- **Proactive DISCOMs.** Distribution companies here have made net-metering applications and approvals comparatively smooth.
- **High awareness.** Widespread adoption means an established installer ecosystem and confident homeowners.

The result is a mature market where residents in Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot and Vadodara routinely install rooftop solar as a standard home upgrade.

## Rooftop solar costs in Ahmedabad (2026)

Turnkey residential systems in 2026 cost about Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW (around Rs 40-55 per watt) using ALMM-listed mono PERC panels. Indicative pre-subsidy prices:

- **1 kW:** Rs 60,000-80,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh
- **3 kW:** Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh
- **5 kW:** Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh
- **10 kW:** Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh

SME and commercial systems, usually larger, are closer to Rs 38-50 per watt. Add roughly Rs 3,000-8,000 for net metering and DISCOM processing, or up to about Rs 15,000 for a three-phase connection. Gujarat's competitive installer market often keeps residential prices at the lower end of these ranges.

## PM Surya Ghar subsidy in Gujarat

The central subsidy applies across Gujarat and stacks on top of any state benefit:

- **1 kW:** Rs 30,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 60,000
- **3 kW and above:** Rs 78,000 (capped)

The slab is Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW plus Rs 18,000 for the third kW, with nothing above 3 kW. Apply at pmsuryaghar.gov.in through an MNRE-empanelled vendor using ALMM-listed modules. The subsidy is credited by DBT about 30-45 days after your DISCOM commissions the system, and eligible households can also access up to 300 free units a month. Where Gujarat offers its own top-up, it applies over and above this central amount.

## Net metering: Torrent Power, UGVCL and DGVCL

Your net-metering application goes to whichever DISCOM bills you:

- **Torrent Power** distributes power in Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Surat urban areas.
- **UGVCL** (Uttar Gujarat Vij Company) serves north Gujarat.
- **DGVCL** (Dakshin Gujarat Vij Company) serves south Gujarat.

Under net metering, the solar power you export by day is credited against the units you import, and you pay only the net. System capacity is generally linked to your sanctioned load. Gujarat's DISCOMs are known for relatively efficient feasibility checks, meter replacement and commissioning, which is a major reason adoption here outpaces most other states.

## Roof feasibility and sizing

- **Space.** Budget roughly 80-100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW; a 3 kW system needs about 250-300 sq ft.
- **Orientation.** South-facing tilted mounting maximises output; flat terraces use tilted frames.
- **Match your bill.** Size to your consumption so exports align with net-metering rules.
- **Heat management.** Gujarat's high temperatures are fine for panels, but good airflow under the modules keeps them efficient.

## A worked example: 3 kW home in Ahmedabad

Consider a bungalow in Ahmedabad on Torrent Power supply:

- **Pre-subsidy cost:** about Rs 1.8 lakh (often at the lower end due to competition)
- **PM Surya Ghar subsidy:** Rs 78,000
- **Net outlay:** about Rs 1.02 lakh (plus ~Rs 5,000 net metering)
- **Generation:** roughly 380 units a month, aided by strong irradiance
- **Bill offset:** savings of around Rs 2,500-3,200 a month, or Rs 30,000-38,000 a year

Payback typically lands around 3 years in Gujarat, at the fast end of the national 3-5 year range, thanks to strong generation and competitive pricing. With a 25-year performance warranty and about 0.5% annual degradation, returns continue for decades.

## Tips for buyers in Gujarat

- Use the state's large installer base to compare **at least three itemised quotes**.
- Confirm **ALMM-listed** panels, **BIS-compliant** equipment and an **MNRE-empanelled** vendor.
- Verify the **25-year performance warranty** and a written maintenance plan.
- Ask the installer to handle the net-metering paperwork end to end.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does Gujarat lead India in rooftop solar?**
A mix of high solar irradiance, strong and consistent state policy, and proactive DISCOMs has driven among the highest rooftop adoption in the country.

**Which DISCOM handles my net metering in Gujarat?**
Torrent Power in Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Surat urban areas; UGVCL in north Gujarat; and DGVCL in south Gujarat. Check your bill for the licensee.

**How much does a 3 kW system cost after subsidy?**
Pre-subsidy Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh, less Rs 78,000, leaves a net outlay near Rs 1 lakh, often at the lower end given Gujarat's competitive market.

**Is the payback faster in Gujarat?**
Often yes, around 3 years, because high irradiance and competitive pricing combine to boost returns.

**Does Gujarat add its own subsidy?**
The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy always applies; where a state top-up is available, it is added on top of the central amount.

**How much will a 3 kW system generate here?**
Roughly 330-420 units a month, frequently at the higher end thanks to Gujarat's strong sun.
