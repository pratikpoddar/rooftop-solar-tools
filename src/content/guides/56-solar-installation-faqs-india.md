## Cost Questions

### How much does rooftop solar cost in India in 2026?
Turnkey residential systems cost about Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW (roughly Rs 40-55 per watt), including ALMM-listed panels, inverter, mounting and installation. Commercial systems are nearer Rs 38-50 per watt.

### What does each system size cost before subsidy?
Indicative pre-subsidy prices: 1 kW Rs 60,000-80,000; 2 kW Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh; 3 kW Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh; 5 kW Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh; 10 kW Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh.

### Are there extra costs beyond the system price?
Yes. Budget roughly Rs 3,000-8,000 for net metering and DISCOM processing, up to about Rs 15,000 for a three-phase connection. There may also be minor costs for structure customisation or cabling runs.

### Can I pay in EMIs instead of upfront?
Yes. Solar loans and EMI schemes are widely available, letting you own the system (and keep the subsidy) while spreading the cost over a few years.

### Is solar cheaper than a fixed deposit as an investment?
For self-consumption, the effective return from bill savings often beats an FD, because a residential system typically pays back in 3-5 years and then delivers near-free power for around two more decades.

## Subsidy Questions

### What is the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?
It is the central rooftop solar scheme launched in February 2024, targeting 1 crore homes, offering a direct subsidy and up to 300 free units a month for eligible households.

### How much subsidy will I get?
Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, and Rs 78,000 for 3 kW and above (capped). The slab is Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 18,000 per kW for the third, with nothing above 3 kW.

### How do I apply for the subsidy?
Register on the national portal at pmsuryaghar.gov.in, select an MNRE-empanelled vendor using ALMM-listed modules, get DISCOM feasibility approval, install, and complete commissioning.

### When is the subsidy paid?
The amount is credited by direct benefit transfer (DBT) to your bank account roughly 30-45 days after your DISCOM inspects and commissions the system.

### Do states add their own subsidy?
Some states offer top-ups over and above the central subsidy. Gujarat, for example, has strong state policy that has helped it lead India in rooftop adoption. Check your state's specific scheme.

## Technical Questions

### How much power will my system generate?
About 4 units per day per kW installed. A 3 kW system produces roughly 330-420 units a month, varying with season, orientation and cleanliness.

### Which type of solar panels should I choose?
Monocrystalline PERC panels are the current standard for Indian homes, offering good efficiency in limited roof space. Ensure they are ALMM-listed and BIS-compliant.

### How long do solar panels last?
Panels typically carry a 25-year performance warranty and degrade only about 0.5% per year, so they keep producing useful power for decades.

### What inverter do I need?
Most homes use a string inverter sized to the system. Hybrid inverters suit setups with batteries, while microinverters can help with partial shading. Match the inverter to your panels and load.

### Do I need a battery?
For grid-connected homes with net metering, a battery is optional. It adds cost and is mainly worthwhile if you face frequent power cuts and want backup.

### How much roof space do I need?
Roughly 80-100 sq ft of shadow-free area per kW. A 3 kW system needs about 250-300 sq ft of clear terrace.

## Installation Questions

### How long does installation take?
The physical installation usually takes a day or two. The full cycle, including DISCOM feasibility, meter change and commissioning, adds a few weeks.

### What are the steps to get solar installed?
Site survey and quotation, portal registration and DISCOM approval, installation of panels and inverter, net-meter inspection, commissioning, and finally subsidy disbursal by DBT.

### What is net metering?
It lets you export surplus daytime solar to the grid and receive credit against the units you import at night, so you are billed only on the net consumption.

### Which DISCOM handles my application?
The company that bills you. For example: Adani/Tata Power/BEST in Mumbai; TGSPDCL in Hyderabad; MSEDCL in Pune; TANGEDCO in Chennai; and Torrent Power/UGVCL/DGVCL in Gujarat.

### How do I choose a good installer?
Pick an MNRE-empanelled vendor using ALMM-listed panels, get an itemised quotation, verify warranties, and compare at least three quotes. Avoid unusually cheap offers that skip quality equipment or DISCOM liaison.

## Maintenance Questions

### How much maintenance does rooftop solar need?
Very little. Regular cleaning to remove dust and periodic checks of wiring and inverter performance are usually enough.

### How often should I clean the panels?
Typically every couple of weeks to monthly, more often in dusty areas, near the coast, or during pollen and bird-dropping seasons. Clean, unshaded panels protect your generation.

### Does the monsoon affect my system?
Generation dips during heavy monsoon spells but recovers strongly afterward. Rain also helps rinse the panels. Size your system for annual output, not peak-month output.

### What if my system underperforms?
Common causes are dirty panels, new shading, loose connections or an inverter fault. Check cleanliness and the inverter display first, then call your installer if output stays low.

### Are panels safe in high heat or storms?
Panels are built for outdoor conditions. In coastal or cyclone-prone areas, insist on corrosion-resistant, wind-rated mounting for extra durability.

## Savings Questions

### How much can I save with rooftop solar?
A 3 kW home system commonly saves Rs 2,300-3,500 a month, or roughly Rs 28,000-42,000 a year, depending on your DISCOM's tariff slabs and your consumption.

### What is the typical payback period?
Residential systems typically pay back in 3-5 years, and often around 3 years in high-tariff cities or high-irradiance states like Gujarat.

### Does solar increase my property value?
A well-installed, subsidised rooftop system can add appeal and value, since buyers inherit lower electricity bills and a long-life asset.

### Is solar a good hedge against tariff hikes?
Yes. As grid tariffs rise, the value of the units you self-generate rises too, so solar effectively locks in a large part of your electricity cost for decades.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to use an MNRE-empanelled vendor?
Yes, if you want the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. Empanelment and ALMM-listed modules are conditions for eligibility.

### Can flat owners in apartments install solar?
Usually only through a housing-society project on the common terrace, since individual flats rarely control roof rights. Independent houses can install directly.

### Will I still get an electricity bill after going solar?
Most likely a much smaller one. Net metering offsets your imports, but fixed charges and any net consumption still appear on the bill.

### Is a 3 kW system enough for an average home?
For many Indian households consuming 300-400 units a month, a 3 kW system covers a large share of usage and captures the full Rs 78,000 subsidy.
