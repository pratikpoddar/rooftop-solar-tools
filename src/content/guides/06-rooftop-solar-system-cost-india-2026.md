# How Much Does a Rooftop Solar System Cost in India in 2026?

Cost is the number one question for anyone considering rooftop solar. The good news for 2026: prices are competitive and government subsidy makes home systems genuinely affordable. This guide breaks down exactly what you will pay, what the subsidy covers, and the extra charges to budget for.

## The headline number: cost per kW in 2026

Residential rooftop solar in India costs roughly **Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW** on a turnkey basis, which works out to about **Rs 40-55 per watt**. Turnkey means the price includes panels, inverter, mounting structure, cabling, installation and standard paperwork.

Commercial systems are cheaper per unit of capacity, at about Rs 38-50 per watt, because of economies of scale, but residential pricing is what most homeowners will see.

## Indicative system prices before subsidy

Here is what different home system sizes cost before subsidy in 2026:

- **1kW:** Rs 60,000-80,000
- **2kW:** Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh
- **3kW:** Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh
- **5kW:** Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh

Prices vary with panel brand, inverter quality, roof complexity and your city. Higher-end panels and sturdier structures cost more but often deliver better long-term value.

## The PM Surya Ghar subsidy: what it knocks off

The central subsidy for residential systems under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana:

- **1kW:** Rs 30,000
- **2kW:** Rs 60,000
- **3kW and above:** Rs 78,000 (capped)

The slab is Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2kW, Rs 18,000 per kW for the 2-3kW band, and nil above 3kW. This is why 3kW is a sweet spot: it captures the full Rs 78,000. Several states add their own top-up on top of the central amount. The subsidy is paid by Direct Benefit Transfer to your bank account, usually 30-45 days after commissioning.

## Net cost after subsidy

Applying the subsidy:

- **1kW:** ~Rs 30,000-50,000 net
- **2kW:** ~Rs 50,000-90,000 net
- **3kW:** ~Rs 90,000-1.3 lakh net
- **5kW:** the subsidy is still capped at Rs 78,000, so net ~Rs 1.7-2.7 lakh

For systems above 3kW, the subsidy stays fixed at Rs 78,000, so the extra capacity is paid at full price.

## The extra charges people forget

The turnkey price is not always the final number. Budget for:

- **Net-metering and DISCOM charges:** roughly Rs 3,000-8,000, and up to about Rs 15,000 for a three-phase connection.
- **Roof preparation:** waterproofing or a raised structure for shade-free mounting can add cost.
- **Higher-tier components:** premium inverters or bifacial panels raise the price.
- **Longer cable runs:** if your meter is far from the array.

Ask your vendor for an all-inclusive written quotation so there are no surprises.

## What drives the price up or down

- **Panel and inverter brand:** Tier-1 ALMM-listed panels and reputable inverters cost more but last and perform better.
- **System size:** Larger systems have a lower per-kW cost.
- **Roof type and access:** Complex or fragile roofs increase labour and structure costs.
- **Phase and location:** Three-phase connections and certain states carry higher DISCOM charges.

## A worked example: a 3kW home in Delhi NCR

A family with a Rs 4,500 monthly bill installs a 3kW system:

- **Turnkey cost:** ~Rs 1.9 lakh (within the Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh band)
- **PM Surya Ghar subsidy:** Rs 78,000
- **Net-metering charges:** ~Rs 5,000
- **Net outlay:** ~Rs 1.17 lakh
- **Generation:** ~330-420 units a month
- **Annual savings:** ~Rs 32,000-40,000
- **Payback:** roughly 3-4 years

After payback, the system keeps generating for two more decades, with panels warranted for around 25 years.

## How to get the best price without cutting corners

- Get at least two or three quotations from MNRE-empanelled vendors.
- Compare on total cost including net-metering, not just per-kW headline.
- Check that panels are ALMM-listed and BIS-compliant.
- Weigh warranty and after-sales service, not price alone.
- Avoid unusually cheap quotes that skip quality components; they cost more later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does a 3kW rooftop solar system cost in India in 2026?**
About Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh before subsidy. After the Rs 78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy, net cost is roughly Rs 90,000-1.3 lakh, plus small net-metering charges.

**What is the cost per kW of rooftop solar?**
Around Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW turnkey for residential systems (about Rs 40-55 per watt). Commercial systems are lower, at roughly Rs 38-50 per watt.

**Are there hidden costs beyond the quotation?**
Yes, mainly net-metering and DISCOM charges of Rs 3,000-8,000 (up to ~Rs 15,000 for three-phase), plus any roof preparation. Ask for an all-inclusive quote.

**Does the subsidy increase for a 5kW system?**
No. The central subsidy is capped at Rs 78,000 for 3kW and above, so a 5kW system receives the same Rs 78,000 as a 3kW one.

**When do I receive the subsidy?**
By Direct Benefit Transfer to your bank account, typically 30-45 days after your DISCOM inspects and commissions the system.

**Is a cheaper quote always a good deal?**
Not necessarily. Very low quotes often use lower-quality panels or inverters, or skip components. Compare warranties, ALMM listing and service before deciding.
