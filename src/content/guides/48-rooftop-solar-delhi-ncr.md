Delhi NCR is one of India's most attractive markets for rooftop solar. The region gets strong year-round sunshine, electricity demand peaks in summer when solar output is highest, and residents can stack the central PM Surya Ghar subsidy with net metering. This guide covers 2026 costs, the subsidy, your DISCOM's net-metering process, and how to get it right.

## Your DISCOM: Who Supplies Your Power

Delhi NCR is served by a few private distribution companies (DISCOMs), and your net-metering application goes through whichever supplies your address:

- **BSES Rajdhani Power Limited (BRPL)** - South and West Delhi
- **BSES Yamuna Power Limited (BYPL)** - Central and East Delhi
- **Tata Power Delhi Distribution Limited (Tata Power-DDL)** - North and North-West Delhi

Check the name on your electricity bill to confirm which one applies. Each runs its own online net-metering portal and inspection process, though the broad steps are similar.

## What Rooftop Solar Costs In Delhi NCR (2026)

For homes, turnkey rooftop solar runs about **Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW** in 2026. Indicative pre-subsidy prices:

- **1 kW:** Rs 60,000-80,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh
- **3 kW:** Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh
- **5 kW:** Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh
- **10 kW:** Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh

Commercial installations are cheaper per watt (~Rs 38-50/Wp) due to scale. Add modest net-metering and DISCOM fees of around Rs 3,000-8,000 (up to ~Rs 15,000 for three-phase connections).

## The PM Surya Ghar Subsidy

Delhi homeowners can claim the central **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana** subsidy for residential systems:

- **Rs 30,000/kW** for the first 2 kW
- **Rs 18,000/kW** for the 2-3 kW band
- **Capped at Rs 78,000** for 3 kW and above

So a 3 kW home system attracts the full Rs 78,000. Apply through the national portal, **pmsuryaghar.gov.in**, using an MNRE-empanelled vendor and ALMM-listed modules. The subsidy is paid by DBT to your bank account, typically around 30-45 days after DISCOM inspection and commissioning. Some states add their own top-ups, so check for any Delhi-specific incentive at the time of applying. Note the subsidy is residential only - businesses instead use accelerated depreciation (~40% p.a.) and 12% GST input credit.

## A Real Example: 3 kW Delhi Home

- **System size:** 3 kW
- **Pre-subsidy cost:** ~Rs 1.9 lakh (mid-range)
- **PM Surya Ghar subsidy:** Rs 78,000
- **Net cost:** ~Rs 1.12 lakh
- **Generation:** ~12 units/day (~360 units/month), based on ~4 units/day per kW
- **Savings:** For a household paying Rs 8/unit on higher slabs, that's roughly Rs 2,880/month, or ~Rs 34,000/year

At that rate, the net cost pays back in around **3-4 years**, comfortably within the typical 3-5 year payback range - after which you enjoy near-free power for the balance of the system's ~25-year life.

## Net Metering In Delhi NCR

Net metering lets you export surplus daytime generation and receive credits against future bills. The process with BSES (BRPL/BYPL) or Tata Power-DDL generally runs:

1. Choose an MNRE-empanelled installer and register on the DISCOM/national portal.
2. Submit the net-metering application with load and system details.
3. Get feasibility approval for your sanctioned load.
4. Install with ALMM-listed panels and a suitable inverter.
5. DISCOM inspection and bi-directional meter installation.
6. Commissioning and net-metering activation; subsidy DBT follows.

Confirm your **sanctioned load** supports the system size, especially above 3-5 kW.

## Best Practices For Delhi NCR

- **Mind the dust and summer heat.** Delhi's dust reduces output, so clean panels regularly - roughly every 2-4 weeks in dry months.
- **Plan for winter smog.** Generation dips in December-January haze; size with the annual average in mind.
- **Use MNRE-empanelled vendors and ALMM modules** - mandatory for the subsidy and better for warranty (mono PERC, ~25-year performance warranty, ~0.5%/year degradation).
- **Get at least three written quotes** and compare price per Wp, brand and net-metering support.
- **Confirm roof rights** if you live in a multi-storey building or society - shared-roof approvals are common in NCR.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which DISCOM handles my solar application in Delhi?**
BSES Rajdhani (South/West), BSES Yamuna (Central/East), or Tata Power-DDL (North/North-West). Check your electricity bill to confirm.

**How much does a 3 kW rooftop system cost in Delhi in 2026?**
Around Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh before subsidy, dropping to roughly Rs 1.1 lakh after the Rs 78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy.

**How much subsidy can I get in Delhi?**
Up to Rs 78,000 for a 3 kW or larger residential system, via PM Surya Ghar - Rs 30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 18,000/kW for the 2-3 kW band.

**When is the subsidy credited?**
By DBT to your bank account, usually around 30-45 days after DISCOM inspection and commissioning.

**What is the payback period in Delhi NCR?**
Typically 3-5 years for homes after subsidy, faster for commercial buildings on higher tariffs.

**Do businesses in Delhi get the PM Surya Ghar subsidy?**
No, it is residential only. Businesses use accelerated depreciation and 12% GST input credit instead.
