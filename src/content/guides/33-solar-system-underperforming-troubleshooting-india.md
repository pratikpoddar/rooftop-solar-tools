# Why Is My Solar System Underperforming? Troubleshooting Guide for India

**SEO Title:** Solar Not Generating Enough? India Fixes
**Meta Description:** Solar system not generating enough in India? Learn the common causes, from dust and shading to inverter faults and net metering, and how to diagnose and fix each.
**Target Keyword:** solar system not generating enough India
**Secondary Keywords:** solar underperforming, low solar generation India, solar inverter error, solar output dropped, solar troubleshooting
**Search Intent:** Informational

You installed rooftop solar expecting your bills to fall, but the generation figures look low, or they were fine and have quietly dropped. Before assuming your panels are faulty, know that most underperformance in Indian homes comes from a handful of simple, fixable causes. This guide helps you diagnose what is going wrong and what to do about it.

## First, Know What "Normal" Looks Like

You cannot spot a problem without a baseline. A well-installed system in India generates roughly **4 units per day per kW** on a clear day. So:

- **1 kW:** about 4 units/day, 120-130 units/month
- **3 kW:** about 12 units/day, 330-420 units/month
- **5 kW:** about 20 units/day, 550-650 units/month

Generation naturally varies with season, cloud cover, and daylight hours. Winter and monsoon days will be lower; summer clear days higher. Judge performance over a month, not a single cloudy day.

Panels also degrade by only about **0.5% per year**, so age is almost never the cause of a sudden drop.

## The Most Common Cause: Dirty Panels

Dust, pollen, pollution, and bird droppings are the number-one reason for lost output in India, cutting generation by **15-25%** in dusty or polluted areas.

- **Symptom:** Gradual decline over dry weeks; a visible film on the glass.
- **Fix:** Clean the panels (early morning, soft brush, plain water). See [How to Clean Solar Panels Safely in India](./32-how-to-clean-solar-panels-india).

If cleaning restores output, dust was your problem. This alone solves a large share of "low generation" complaints.

## Shading You May Not Have Noticed

Even partial shade on one panel can drag down an entire string, because panels in a string are linked.

- **Symptom:** Output dips at specific times of day (morning or afternoon).
- **Causes:** A tree that has grown, a new water tank, a dish antenna, a neighbour's construction, or even a chimney.
- **Fix:** Trim branches, relocate the object if possible, or ask your installer about re-wiring, panel-level optimisers, or microinverters to isolate the affected panel.

## Inverter Problems

The inverter converts DC to AC and is the component most likely to fault.

- **Symptom:** Inverter display shows an error code, no output, or output far below expected. Frequent tripping or shutdowns.
- **Common issues:** Overheating (poor ventilation), grid voltage out of range, a blown fuse, or an aging inverter near the end of its 5-10 year warranty.
- **Fix:** Note the error code and call your installer. Keep the inverter cool, shaded, and dust-free. Overheating in Indian summers is a frequent, avoidable cause.

## Grid and DISCOM Issues

Grid-tied systems shut down when the grid is unstable or absent, by design (anti-islanding), so power fluctuations reduce your generation.

- **Symptom:** Inverter shows grid-related errors; output stops during outages or voltage swings.
- **Fix:** This is a supply-side issue. Persistent high grid voltage should be reported to your DISCOM. A hybrid inverter with a battery can keep you running through outages, but adds cost.

## Net Metering Not Working

Sometimes the panels are generating fine, but your bill does not reflect the savings.

- **Symptom:** Good generation figures on the inverter app, but the electricity bill still shows no export credit.
- **Causes:** The bidirectional net meter was never installed, is faulty, or the DISCOM has not activated your net-metering account.
- **Fix:** Confirm the system was fully commissioned and the net meter is bidirectional and recording exports. Follow up with your installer and DISCOM. Remember net-metering charges of Rs 3,000-8,000 (up to about Rs 15,000 three-phase) apply.

## Wiring, Connectors, and Fuses

Loose or corroded connections cause resistance, heat, and lost output.

- **Symptom:** Output lower than expected with no dust or shading; discoloured or warm connectors.
- **Fix:** This needs a technician. Do not open junction boxes yourself. Rodent-chewed cables are also common on Indian rooftops.

## Wrong Expectations or Under-Sizing

Sometimes the system is fine but was undersized for the home's needs, or the homeowner expected unrealistic numbers.

- **Symptom:** Bills fell but not as much as hoped.
- **Fix:** Compare actual generation to the 4 units/day per kW benchmark. If the system meets that, it is working; the issue is sizing. You may need to add capacity. See [What Size Solar System Do You Need in India](./21-what-size-solar-system-india-sizing-guide).

## A Step-by-Step Diagnosis

Work through these in order:

1. **Check the season and weather.** Are recent days cloudy or short (winter/monsoon)? Compare over a month.
2. **Look at the panels.** Visible dust? Clean them and recheck.
3. **Check for new shading.** Any new object or grown tree casting shade?
4. **Read the inverter.** Any error codes? Is it running during daylight?
5. **Compare to benchmark.** Is daily output near 4 units/kW on clear days?
6. **Check net metering.** Are exports showing on your bill?
7. **Still low? Call your installer.** Likely wiring, connector, or inverter fault needing a technician.

## A Worked Example

A Pune homeowner with a 5 kW system saw output fall from about 20 to 14 units a day (a 30% drop). Steps: weather was clear, so not seasonal. The panels had a heavy dust film after dry weeks; cleaning recovered about 15%. The remaining loss traced to a tree branch newly shading two panels in the afternoon; trimming it restored full output. Total fix cost: a morning of cleaning and Rs 500 for tree trimming.

## When to Escalate

Call your installer or invoke the warranty if:

- Output stays well below benchmark after cleaning and clearing shade
- The inverter shows persistent error codes
- A panel is visibly cracked, discoloured, or has hot spots
- Net metering is not crediting exports despite good generation

Your panels carry a performance warranty of around 25 years, and the inverter 5-10 years, so genuine hardware faults should be covered.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much electricity should my solar system generate in India?**
About 4 units per day per kW on a clear day. A 3 kW system should produce roughly 330-420 units a month; a 5 kW system around 550-650 units.

**Why did my solar generation suddenly drop?**
The most common causes are dirty panels and new shading, followed by inverter faults. Clean the panels and check for shade first, then read the inverter for error codes.

**Is lower output in winter or monsoon a problem?**
No. Shorter days and cloud cover naturally reduce output in winter and the monsoon. Judge performance over a full month against clear-day benchmarks.

**My inverter shows an error code. What should I do?**
Note the exact code and contact your installer. Common causes are overheating, grid voltage issues, or a fuse. Keep the inverter shaded, ventilated, and dust-free.

**My panels generate well but my bill hasn't dropped. Why?**
Your net meter may not be installed, activated, or recording exports. Confirm the system is fully commissioned with a working bidirectional meter, and follow up with your DISCOM.

**Could old panels be the reason for low output?**
Unlikely. Panels degrade only about 0.5% per year, so after several years they still produce over 95% of original output. Sudden drops point to dust, shade, or a fault, not age.
