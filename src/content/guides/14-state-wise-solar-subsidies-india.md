# State-Wise Solar Subsidies in India: A Complete Comparison

The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana provides a uniform central subsidy across India, but your total benefit can vary depending on which state you live in. Several states offer **additional top-up subsidies** or their own net-metering and DISCOM rules that affect the economics of rooftop solar. This guide explains how the two layers work together and how to find your state-specific benefit in 2026.

## Two Layers of Subsidy: Central + State

Rooftop solar support in India works in two layers:

1. **Central subsidy (PM Surya Ghar):** Uniform nationwide, paid via Direct Benefit Transfer.
   - Rs 30,000 for 1 kW
   - Rs 60,000 for 2 kW
   - Rs 78,000 for 3 kW and above (capped)
2. **State top-up (where offered):** Some state governments or DISCOMs add their own subsidy on top of the central amount, increasing your net benefit.

So two households installing identical 3 kW systems can end up with different net costs purely because one lives in a state with a top-up.

## How the Central Subsidy Stays Constant

Regardless of your state, the central slab logic is the same:

- Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW
- Rs 18,000 per kW for the 2-3 kW band
- Nil above 3 kW

The maximum central grant is **Rs 78,000**, and it is administered through the single national portal, **pmsuryaghar.gov.in**. This consistency is deliberate: it makes the base benefit predictable across the country.

## What Varies by State

While the central subsidy is fixed, several state-level factors change your real-world outcome:

### 1. Additional state top-up subsidies

Some states have historically offered extra capital subsidies for residential rooftop solar, stacking on top of the central benefit. These programmes change over time and by budget cycle, so the presence and size of a top-up must be verified for the current year.

### 2. Net-metering rules

Each state's electricity regulator and DISCOM set net-metering policy:

- System-size caps relative to sanctioned load
- Whether net metering or gross/net billing applies at larger sizes
- How surplus export credits carry forward or settle

These rules directly affect how much of your generation you can monetise.

### 3. DISCOM fees and processes

Net-meter costs (Rs 3,000-8,000, up to ~Rs 15,000 for three-phase), inspection timelines, and load-enhancement charges differ between utilities.

### 4. Electricity tariffs

Tariffs vary widely by state and consumer slab. A higher tariff means faster payback, so the same system pays back sooner in a high-tariff state than a low-tariff one.

## How State Differences Change the Maths

Consider two homeowners installing 3 kW systems, both receiving the Rs 78,000 central subsidy:

- **Homeowner A (state with a top-up):** Pre-subsidy cost ~Rs 1.9 lakh, less Rs 78,000 central and, say, an additional state top-up, giving a lower net cost and a shorter payback.
- **Homeowner B (no top-up, but high tariff):** Same central subsidy, no state add-on, but a tariff of ~Rs 9/unit means larger monthly savings, so payback still lands comfortably in the 3-5 year range.

The lesson: your net benefit is a combination of the central subsidy, any state top-up, your tariff, and local DISCOM rules, not just one number.

## States and Union Territories: General Patterns

Because state programmes and budgets change frequently, treat the following as patterns to verify rather than fixed figures:

- **Solar-forward states** (for example, those with strong rooftop targets and established DISCOM processes) often have smoother net-metering approvals and, at times, extra incentives.
- **High-tariff urban states/metros** deliver quick payback even without a top-up because avoided electricity is worth more.
- **States with lower residential tariffs** rely more on the central subsidy for attractive economics.

For the current status in your state, always confirm on the official portal and with your local DISCOM before committing.

## How to Check Your State's Benefit

1. **Visit pmsuryaghar.gov.in**, select your state and DISCOM during registration.
2. **Check your DISCOM's website or office** for current net-metering policy and any state top-up.
3. **Ask MNRE-empanelled vendors** in your area, they handle these applications daily and know local specifics.
4. **Read your electricity bill** to note your per-unit tariff, which drives your savings and payback.

## Practical Takeaways

- The **central Rs 78,000 cap** is the same everywhere; state top-ups (where they exist) are a bonus.
- **Net-metering and tariff rules matter as much as the subsidy** for your overall return.
- Use **ALMM-listed modules** and an **MNRE-empanelled vendor** everywhere, this is required to claim the central subsidy regardless of state.
- Verify current figures; **state programmes and budgets change** year to year.
- Even without a state top-up, a residential payback of **3-5 years** is typical thanks to the central subsidy and net metering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do all Indian states offer extra solar subsidies?**
No. The central PM Surya Ghar subsidy (up to Rs 78,000) is uniform nationwide, but only some states add their own top-up. Verify your state's current programme before assuming a top-up applies.

**Is the central subsidy amount different in different states?**
No. It is fixed nationally: Rs 30,000 (1 kW), Rs 60,000 (2 kW), Rs 78,000 (3 kW and above), administered through pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

**Why does payback differ between states even with the same subsidy?**
Because electricity tariffs, net-metering rules, and DISCOM fees vary. A higher tariff means larger savings and faster payback for the same system.

**Where can I confirm my state's rooftop solar rules?**
On pmsuryaghar.gov.in after selecting your DISCOM, on your DISCOM's website, or from an MNRE-empanelled vendor operating in your area.

**Does the state I live in change the compliance requirements?**
The core requirements, ALMM-listed modules and an MNRE-empanelled vendor, apply everywhere to claim the central subsidy. Net-metering specifics differ by state.

**Can a state top-up meaningfully lower my cost?**
Yes, where offered, a top-up stacks on the central subsidy and reduces net cost further, shortening payback. Confirm the current amount for your state.
