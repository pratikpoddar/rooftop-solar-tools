# Red Flags to Avoid When Hiring a Solar Company in India

**Search Intent:** Informational

India's rooftop solar boom has attracted honest EPC firms and, unfortunately, opportunists who cut corners or cheat outright. Because a solar system is a 25-year, lakh-plus investment tied to government subsidies, a bad vendor can cost you far more than the sticker price. This guide lists the warning signs so you can walk away before you lose money.

## Why Scams Thrive in Solar

Three factors make solar buyers vulnerable:

- **Subsidies** create an incentive for fake promises and false "government agent" claims.
- **Technical jargon** (ALMM, kW, net metering) lets dishonest vendors bluff.
- **Long warranties** are easy to promise and hard to enforce if the company vanishes.

Knowing the red flags is your best defence.

## Red Flag 1: Fake or "Guaranteed Overnight" Subsidy Claims

Be extremely cautious if a vendor:

- Claims to be an official government agent collecting money for the subsidy.
- Promises the subsidy will be paid before installation or inspection.
- Asks you to pay the subsidy amount to them and "get reimbursed."

The reality: the PM Surya Ghar central subsidy is paid **directly to your bank account via DBT**, typically **30-45 days after DISCOM inspection and commissioning**. It is never routed through the vendor's account, and it never arrives before commissioning. Amounts are fixed: **Rs 30,000 (1 kW), Rs 60,000 (2 kW), Rs 78,000 (3 kW and above)**. Anyone promising a different figure or a faster back-channel payout is misleading you.

## Red Flag 2: Non-ALMM Panels or Vague Equipment

If the vendor will not name the exact panel make, model, and wattage, or dodges questions about ALMM listing, treat it as a serious warning. Only **ALMM-listed, BIS-compliant modules** qualify for the subsidy. Using off-list panels can:

- Disqualify you from the entire central subsidy.
- Leave you with low-quality, poorly warrantied hardware.

Always insist on written model numbers and verify ALMM status yourself.

## Red Flag 3: Not MNRE-Empanelled

For the residential subsidy you must use an **MNRE-empanelled vendor** listed for your DISCOM on pmsuryaghar.gov.in. A vendor who is not empanelled but insists you will "still get the subsidy" is either uninformed or lying. Verify their empanelment directly on the portal, not from a screenshot they send you.

## Red Flag 4: Prices Too Good to Be True

Residential turnkey pricing in 2026 is roughly **Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW**. A quote far below this range almost always means:

- Non-ALMM or low-tier panels.
- Thin, unsafe cabling and a weak mounting structure.
- No net-metering or paperwork support.
- Hidden costs added later.

A 3 kW system quoted at, say, Rs 1.1 lakh when the honest range is Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh should trigger hard questions, not excitement.

## Red Flag 5: Demanding Full Payment Upfront

Reputable installers use a **staged payment schedule** tied to milestones (advance, on delivery, on installation, on commissioning). A demand for 100% payment before any work, or pressure to pay in cash to a personal account, is a classic scam signal. Never pay the full amount before equipment is delivered and installed.

## Red Flag 6: Inflated Savings and Generation Promises

Realistic generation is about **4 units per kW per day**, so a 3 kW system produces roughly **330-420 units per month**. Be skeptical of anyone who:

- Promises far higher output than your city's irradiance supports.
- Claims your bill will hit exactly zero (fixed/demand charges usually remain).
- Guarantees a 1-2 year payback (typical residential payback is **3-5 years**).

Overpromising on savings is how weak systems get sold.

## Red Flag 7: No Written Warranty or Contract

Verbal assurances are worthless when a company disappears. Insist on written documentation of:

- Panel performance warranty (around 25 years, ~0.5%/year degradation).
- Inverter warranty (typically 5-10 years).
- Workmanship warranty and after-sales terms.
- A signed contract with scope, timeline, and payment stages.

No paperwork means no recourse.

## Red Flag 8: No Local Presence or References

A 25-year asset needs a vendor who will still be reachable. Warning signs include:

- No verifiable office or local service team.
- Refusal to share recent customer references in your city.
- Only a phone number and no registered company details.

Call a couple of past customers before you commit.

## Red Flag 9: Pressure Tactics and "Limited-Time" Offers

Scammers rush you so you cannot verify claims. Be wary of "subsidy ending today," "last 2 systems at this price," or demands to sign on the first visit. Legitimate subsidies and pricing do not vanish overnight, and a genuine vendor will let you compare three quotes.

## A Simple Self-Protection Checklist

Before paying anything, confirm:

- [ ] Vendor is MNRE-empanelled for your DISCOM (checked on the portal).
- [ ] Panels are ALMM-listed with named model and wattage.
- [ ] Price is within the Rs 55,000-70,000/kW range.
- [ ] Payment is staged, to a registered company account.
- [ ] Subsidy is via DBT to your bank after commissioning, not through the vendor.
- [ ] Written warranties, contract, and timeline are provided.
- [ ] Local references and service support exist.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if a solar company is a scam?
Watch for fake subsidy claims, unnamed or non-ALMM panels, prices far below Rs 55,000-70,000/kW, full-upfront cash demands, and inflated savings promises. Verify empanelment on pmsuryaghar.gov.in.

### Is the solar subsidy ever paid through the installer?
No. The central subsidy is paid via DBT directly to your bank account, typically 30-45 days after DISCOM inspection. Anyone collecting the subsidy on your behalf is a red flag.

### What if a vendor offers a much cheaper price?
A quote well below the honest range usually means non-ALMM panels, unsafe cabling, or hidden costs. Cheap often disqualifies your subsidy and costs more over time.

### Can I trust a "limited-time subsidy" offer?
No. Subsidy amounts and eligibility follow published rules and do not expire overnight. Pressure tactics are a sign to slow down and verify.

### What documents protect me from fraud?
A signed contract with staged payments, written panel and inverter warranties, ALMM model details, and proof of MNRE empanelment. Without these, you have little recourse.
