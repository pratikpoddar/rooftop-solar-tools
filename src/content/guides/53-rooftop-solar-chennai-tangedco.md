Chennai's abundant sunshine and high summer cooling loads make rooftop solar a compelling way to control electricity bills. Independent homes across T. Nagar, Adyar, Velachery and the OMR corridor usually have the flat terraces solar needs, and Tamil Nadu's DISCOM has a defined net-metering process. This guide covers 2026 costs, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, TANGEDCO rules and practical tips for the coastal climate.

## Why solar works in Chennai

- **High irradiance.** Chennai gets strong sun for most of the year, delivering roughly 4 units per day per kW installed.
- **Heavy AC use.** Long, hot summers mean big cooling bills that solar can offset directly.
- **Flat terraces.** Many independent houses and small commercial buildings have open, load-bearing roofs.
- **Central subsidy.** PM Surya Ghar lowers the upfront cost for residential systems.

A 3 kW system in Chennai typically generates about 330-420 units a month, a large share of an average home's consumption.

## Rooftop solar costs in Chennai (2026)

Turnkey residential systems in 2026 cost about Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW (around Rs 40-55 per watt) with ALMM-listed mono PERC panels and a good inverter. Indicative pre-subsidy prices:

- **1 kW:** Rs 60,000-80,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh
- **3 kW:** Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh
- **5 kW:** Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh
- **10 kW:** Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh

SME and commercial systems are usually nearer Rs 38-50 per watt. Budget roughly Rs 3,000-8,000 for net metering and DISCOM processing, or up to about Rs 15,000 for a three-phase connection.

## PM Surya Ghar subsidy in Chennai

The central subsidy applies to residential systems across Tamil Nadu:

- **1 kW:** Rs 30,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 60,000
- **3 kW and above:** Rs 78,000 (capped)

The slab is Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 18,000 per kW for the third, with nothing above 3 kW. Apply at pmsuryaghar.gov.in through an MNRE-empanelled vendor using ALMM-listed modules. The subsidy is credited by DBT to your bank account about 30-45 days after TANGEDCO commissions the system. Eligible households can also receive up to 300 free units a month; any Tamil Nadu state incentive would be additional.

## Net metering with TANGEDCO

Chennai is served by TANGEDCO (Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation). Under its net-metering framework:

- Daytime solar exports are set off against the units you import, and you are billed on the net.
- Capacity is generally linked to your sanctioned load, so match system size to your connection.
- Three-phase homes and businesses pay slightly higher metering and processing charges.
- Apply for net metering along with your subsidy application; TANGEDCO inspects the site and installs a bidirectional meter before commissioning.

Keep your latest bill, service-connection number, sanctioned-load details and ownership documents ready for both processes.

## Coastal climate: practical tips

Chennai's seaside location calls for a few extra precautions:

- **Salt and humidity.** Near the coast, choose corrosion-resistant mounting structures and hardware.
- **More frequent cleaning.** Salt, dust and pollen build up faster; clean panels regularly to protect output.
- **Cyclone readiness.** Insist on robust, properly anchored mounting rated for high wind speeds.
- **Monsoon.** The northeast monsoon reduces generation for a spell; size for annual output, not just peak months.

## A worked example: 3 kW home in Velachery

Take a family in Velachery on TANGEDCO supply with heavy summer AC use:

- **Pre-subsidy cost:** about Rs 1.85 lakh
- **PM Surya Ghar subsidy:** Rs 78,000
- **Net outlay:** about Rs 1.07 lakh (plus ~Rs 5,000 net metering)
- **Generation:** roughly 360 units a month
- **Bill offset:** at prevailing slab rates, savings of around Rs 2,300-3,000 a month, or Rs 28,000-36,000 a year

That puts payback in the 3-5 year range. With a 25-year performance warranty and about 0.5% annual degradation, the system continues to cut bills for decades.

## Choosing a good installer

- Insist on **ALMM-listed** panels and **BIS-compliant** equipment.
- Use an **MNRE-empanelled vendor** to protect your subsidy eligibility.
- Get an itemised quotation with panel make, inverter model, warranty terms and net-metering handling.
- For coastal sites, confirm corrosion-resistant mounting and a wind-rated structure.
- Be cautious of quotes well below market that skimp on quality or omit DISCOM liaison.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which DISCOM handles rooftop solar in Chennai?**
TANGEDCO processes net-metering applications and installs the bidirectional meter across Chennai and Tamil Nadu.

**How much does a 3 kW system cost after subsidy?**
Roughly Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh before subsidy, less Rs 78,000, leaving a net outlay near Rs 1 lakh plus small net-metering charges.

**How much power will it generate?**
About 4 units per day per kW, so a 3 kW system produces roughly 330-420 units a month depending on season and cleanliness.

**Is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy available in Tamil Nadu?**
Yes. The central subsidy of up to Rs 78,000 applies; apply via pmsuryaghar.gov.in using an empanelled vendor.

**Does coastal air harm panels?**
Panels are built for the outdoors, but near the sea use corrosion-resistant mounting and clean more often to remove salt.

**What is the payback period in Chennai?**
Typically 3-5 years for residential systems, helped by strong generation and high summer cooling loads.
