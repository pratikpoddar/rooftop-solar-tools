# How to Apply for Rooftop Solar Subsidy in India: Step-by-Step

Applying for the rooftop solar subsidy in India is more straightforward than most people expect. The entire process runs through the national portal, **pmsuryaghar.gov.in**, which connects you, your DISCOM, and an MNRE-empanelled vendor. This guide walks you through each step, from registration to receiving the subsidy in your bank account.

## Before You Start: What You Need

Have these ready to make the process smooth:

- Your **electricity bill** (for consumer number and DISCOM details)
- A **mobile number and email** for the portal login
- **Bank account details** for the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Basic **roof details** (available area, single or three-phase connection)

You must be the consumer of record (or authorised), and you should not have previously claimed a rooftop solar subsidy for the same premises.

## Step 1: Register on the PM Surya Ghar Portal

Go to **pmsuryaghar.gov.in** and register:

1. Select your **state** and **DISCOM**.
2. Enter your **electricity consumer number** (from your bill).
3. Provide your **mobile number and email**, then verify via OTP.
4. Log in to the consumer dashboard.

This creates your account and links your application to the correct DISCOM.

## Step 2: Submit Your Rooftop Solar Application

Inside the portal, apply for rooftop solar by entering:

- The **system capacity** you want (e.g., 1kW, 2kW, 3kW, 5kW)
- Basic details about your **connection and roof**

Remember the subsidy is capped at Rs 78,000 for systems of 3kW and above. Choose capacity based on your consumption; a 3kW system generates roughly 330-420 units/month at about 4 units/day per kW.

## Step 3: Wait for DISCOM Feasibility Approval

Your DISCOM reviews the application for **technical feasibility**, confirming your connection can support the proposed system and net metering. This is usually quick. You will be notified in the portal once approved.

Do not begin installation before this approval, doing so can jeopardise your subsidy.

## Step 4: Choose an MNRE-Empanelled Vendor

Once approved, select a **registered (MNRE-empanelled) vendor** from the list available in the portal for your area. This is critical: to claim the subsidy you must use an empanelled vendor and **ALMM-listed modules**. Compare:

- Itemised, all-inclusive quotes (panels, inverter, structure, cabling, net meter, liaison)
- Warranty terms (panels carry ~25-year performance warranties)
- Reviews and track record

Sign the contract and finalise pricing with your chosen vendor.

## Step 5: Get the System Installed

The vendor installs the system, typically standard mono PERC panels on a mounting structure sized for your roof. Ensure the installation uses:

- **ALMM-listed modules** and BIS-compliant components
- Proper **earthing and safety gear**

Installation itself usually takes a few days once materials are on site; the overall timeline from application to commissioning is longer because of approvals and inspection.

## Step 6: Apply for Net Metering and DISCOM Inspection

After installation, submit the details in the portal and request:

1. **Net-meter installation** (bidirectional meter). Budget Rs 3,000-8,000, up to ~Rs 15,000 for three-phase.
2. A **DISCOM inspection** of the completed system.

The DISCOM verifies the installation meets standards and installs the net meter. Once satisfied, they **commission** the system and issue a commissioning certificate.

## Step 7: Submit Bank Details and Receive the Subsidy

After commissioning:

1. Upload your **commissioning certificate** and **bank account details** in the portal.
2. The subsidy is disbursed via **Direct Benefit Transfer** to your account, typically **30-45 days after inspection/commissioning**.

You receive the applicable amount, Rs 30,000 (1kW), Rs 60,000 (2kW), or Rs 78,000 (3kW and above), plus any state top-up if your state offers one.

## A Realistic Timeline

While each step can be fast, approvals and scheduling add up. A typical end-to-end journey:

- Registration and application: a few days
- Feasibility approval: days to a couple of weeks
- Vendor selection and installation: 1-3 weeks
- Net meter, inspection, commissioning: 1-3 weeks
- Subsidy DBT: 30-45 days after commissioning

Plan for a couple of months overall, and remember you fund the system upfront and receive the subsidy as a reimbursement afterwards.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Installing before feasibility approval**, which can void eligibility.
- **Using a non-empanelled vendor** or non-ALMM panels, disqualifying the subsidy.
- **Oversizing expecting more subsidy**, the grant is capped at Rs 78,000.
- **Forgetting DISCOM/net-metering fees** in your budget.
- **Not planning cash flow** for the 30-45 day subsidy lag.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where do I apply for the rooftop solar subsidy?**
On the national portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in. It links you to your DISCOM and to registered (MNRE-empanelled) vendors.

**Can I choose any solar installer?**
No. To claim the subsidy you must use an MNRE-empanelled vendor and ALMM-listed modules. The portal lists approved vendors for your area.

**Do I need to pay for the system first?**
Yes. You pay (or finance) the full cost upfront. The subsidy is reimbursed via DBT, usually 30-45 days after commissioning.

**How long does the whole process take?**
Often around two months end to end, covering application, feasibility approval, installation, inspection, commissioning, and the subsidy transfer.

**What if I install a bigger system than 3kW?**
You can, but the central subsidy is capped at Rs 78,000 (3kW and above). Extra capacity gets no additional central subsidy.

**Will I get free electricity after this?**
A rightly-sized system plus net metering can bring bills to near zero, up to 300 free units/month for eligible households, by offsetting grid consumption.
