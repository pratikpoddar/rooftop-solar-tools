- PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana central subsidy: Rs 30,000 (1 kW), Rs 60,000 (2 kW), Rs 78,000 (3 kW and above, capped). Structure: Rs 30,000/kW for first 2 kW, Rs 18,000/kW for the 2–3 kW band, nil above 3 kW. - Typical payback: 3–5 years residential.
