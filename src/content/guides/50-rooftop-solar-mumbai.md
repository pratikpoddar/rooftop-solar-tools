Mumbai combines two things that make rooftop solar attractive: some of India's highest retail electricity tariffs and reliable year-round sun. If you own a bungalow, a top-floor flat with roof rights, or run a shop or small office, a rooftop system can cut your bill sharply and pay for itself in a few years. This guide covers 2026 costs, the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, and how net metering works across Mumbai's three distribution companies.

## Why rooftop solar makes sense in Mumbai

- **High tariffs.** Mumbai's higher consumption slabs are among the costliest in India, so every unit your panels generate offsets an expensive grid unit.
- **Good generation.** A well-oriented system produces roughly 4 units per day per kW installed. A 3 kW system yields about 330-420 units a month.
- **Space-efficient options.** Even a compact 1-2 kW system meaningfully dents a mid-size home bill.
- **Subsidy support.** The central PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana gives residential users a direct benefit transfer to your bank account.

## What rooftop solar costs in Mumbai (2026)

Turnkey residential prices in 2026 run about Rs 55,000-70,000 per kW (roughly Rs 40-55 per watt), including ALMM-listed mono PERC panels, inverter, mounting, wiring and installation. Indicative pre-subsidy costs:

- **1 kW:** Rs 60,000-80,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 1.1-1.5 lakh
- **3 kW:** Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh
- **5 kW:** Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh
- **10 kW:** Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh

Commercial and SME installations, which are usually larger, land nearer Rs 38-50 per watt. Budget an additional Rs 3,000-8,000 for net-metering and DISCOM processing (up to about Rs 15,000 for a three-phase connection).

## PM Surya Ghar subsidy for Mumbai homes

The central subsidy is fixed nationwide and applies in Mumbai:

- **1 kW:** Rs 30,000
- **2 kW:** Rs 60,000
- **3 kW and above:** Rs 78,000 (capped)

The slab works out to Rs 30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and Rs 18,000 per kW for the third kW, with nothing above 3 kW. Apply on the national portal at pmsuryaghar.gov.in, use an MNRE-empanelled vendor and ALMM-listed modules, and the subsidy is paid by DBT to your bank account roughly 30-45 days after your DISCOM inspects and commissions the system. Eligible households can also access up to 300 free units a month.

## Net metering in Mumbai: Adani, Tata Power and BEST

Mumbai is served by three licensees, and your net-metering application goes to whichever one bills you:

- **Adani Electricity** covers most of the western and central suburbs (Andheri, Borivali, Mulund and beyond).
- **Tata Power** serves parts of the city and offers supply across several areas.
- **BEST** distributes power in the island city and South Mumbai.

Under net metering, the surplus your system exports during the day is credited against the units you import at night, and you pay only for the net consumption. Regulations are set by the Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC). Practical points for Mumbai:

- Your rooftop capacity is typically capped in relation to your sanctioned load, so match system size to your connection.
- Three-phase homes and SMEs have slightly higher connection and metering charges.
- Credits usually carry forward within the settlement period rather than being paid out in cash.

## Is your roof feasible? Mumbai realities

Mumbai's dense, vertical housing creates specific challenges:

- **Flats and societies.** Individual flat owners rarely control the terrace. Most residential solar here is done as a **housing society** project on common rooftops, either for common-area loads or shared among members. You will need society and managing committee approval.
- **Shadow-free area.** You need roughly 80-100 sq ft of unshaded roof per kW. Neighbouring high-rises can throw shadows for part of the day.
- **Coastal air.** Near the sea, choose corrosion-resistant mounting and clean panels more often to remove salt and grime.
- **Monsoon.** Generation dips in July-August but recovers strongly; size for annual output, not peak-month output.

## A worked example: 3 kW home in Andheri

Consider a bungalow or top-floor flat in Andheri on Adani Electricity:

- **Pre-subsidy cost:** about Rs 1.9 lakh
- **PM Surya Ghar subsidy:** Rs 78,000
- **Net outlay:** about Rs 1.12 lakh (plus ~Rs 5,000 net-metering)
- **Generation:** roughly 360 units a month
- **Bill offset:** at high Mumbai slab rates, that can save Rs 3,000-3,500 a month, or Rs 36,000-42,000 a year

At those savings, payback lands around 3 years, well inside the typical 3-5 year residential range. Over a 25-year panel life with about 0.5% annual degradation, the system keeps delivering value long after it has paid for itself.

## Choosing an installer in Mumbai

- Insist on **ALMM-listed** panels and **BIS-compliant** equipment.
- Use an **MNRE-empanelled vendor** so you stay subsidy-eligible.
- Get an itemised quotation listing panel make, inverter model, warranty terms and net-metering handling.
- Confirm a **25-year performance warranty** on panels and a written maintenance plan.
- Be wary of unusually low quotes that skip ALMM modules or omit DISCOM liaison.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I install solar if I live in a flat?**
Usually only the society controls the terrace, so most flat-dweller installations happen as a society-level project. Individual bungalows and top-floor units with roof rights can install directly.

**Which DISCOM handles my net metering?**
It depends on your address: Adani Electricity in most suburbs, Tata Power in several areas, and BEST in South Mumbai and the island city. Check your electricity bill for the licensee name.

**How much can a 3 kW system save in Mumbai?**
Around 330-420 units a month, which at Mumbai's high slab rates can be Rs 3,000-3,500 monthly, or roughly Rs 36,000-42,000 a year.

**Is the PM Surya Ghar subsidy available in Mumbai?**
Yes. The central subsidy of up to Rs 78,000 (for 3 kW and above) applies. Apply via pmsuryaghar.gov.in using an empanelled vendor.

**Does salty coastal air damage panels?**
Panels are built for outdoor life, but near the coast use corrosion-resistant mounting and clean more frequently to remove salt deposits.

**How long does installation take?**
Physical installation is quick, but DISCOM approval, meter change and commissioning add a few weeks. Plan for the full cycle before the subsidy is disbursed.
