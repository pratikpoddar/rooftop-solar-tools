# Solar for Shops, Clinics & Offices in India: Sizing and Costs

**SEO Title:** Solar for Shops, Clinics & Offices India
**Meta Description:** Sizing and 2026 costs for rooftop solar on Indian shops, clinics and offices - system sizes, prices in Rs, savings, roof space and payback for small businesses.
**Target Keyword:** solar for shops and offices India
**Secondary Keywords:** solar for clinic India, small business solar cost, office rooftop solar, solar system size for shop, commercial solar sizing India
**Search Intent:** Commercial

Shops, clinics and offices are ideal candidates for rooftop solar. Their electricity use peaks during the day - lighting, ACs, computers, medical equipment, refrigeration - exactly when solar produces. They pay high commercial tariffs of Rs 8-12 per unit, so every solar unit saves a lot. And they usually need modest, easy-to-install systems. This guide shows how to size and cost a system for a typical small business in 2026.

## Why These Businesses Are A Perfect Fit

- **Daytime-heavy load** means high self-consumption and fast payback.
- **High commercial tariffs (Rs 8-12/unit)** make each displaced unit valuable - payback is often faster than for homes.
- **Tax efficiency:** businesses can claim accelerated depreciation (~40% p.a.) and 12% GST input credit.
- **Modest sizes** (3-25 kW) that fit most rooftops and terraces.

Remember: the PM Surya Ghar central subsidy is for residential homes only, so shop, clinic and office economics rest on tariffs, depreciation and GST - not that subsidy.

## Step 1: Size From Your Bill

A rooftop system generates about **4 units per day per kW**. To size correctly:

1. Take your average monthly units from 12 months of bills.
2. Estimate how much of that runs during daylight hours (usually most of it for shops, clinics and offices).
3. Divide the daytime monthly units by ~120 (units per kW per month) to get the kW you need.

For example, a business using ~1,800 daytime units/month needs roughly 15 kW (1,800 / 120).

## Step 2: Match Size To Business Type

Typical sizes and 2026 pre-subsidy costs (commercial turnkey ~Rs 38-50/Wp):

- **Small shop / boutique (3 kW):** ~Rs 1.65-2.1 lakh. Generates ~12 units/day. Covers lighting, fans, a couple of ACs.
- **Clinic / small diagnostic centre (5 kW):** ~Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh. Generates ~20 units/day. Runs equipment, ACs, refrigeration.
- **Mid-size office (10 kW):** ~Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh. Generates ~40 units/day. Covers a floor of workstations and ACs.
- **Showroom / larger office (25 kW):** ~Rs 10-14 lakh. Generates ~100 units/day. Covers heavy AC and lighting loads.

## Step 3: Check Roof Space

You need roughly **80-100 sq ft of shadow-free roof per kW**:

- 3 kW: ~250-300 sq ft
- 5 kW: ~400-500 sq ft
- 10 kW: ~800-1,000 sq ft
- 25 kW: ~2,000-2,500 sq ft

Rented premises need the landlord's written permission. Elevated mounting structures can free up usable terrace space beneath the panels.

## Step 4: Estimate Savings And Payback

Worked example - a **5 kW clinic**:

- Cost: ~Rs 3 lakh (mid-range)
- Generation: ~20 units/day = ~600 units/month
- Tariff displaced: Rs 10/unit
- Monthly saving: 600 x Rs 10 = **Rs 6,000**
- Annual saving: **~Rs 72,000**
- Simple payback: **~4 years**, before tax benefits

Add accelerated depreciation and GST input credit and the effective payback shortens further, landing in the typical **3-5 year** range. After payback, the system delivers near-free power for the rest of its ~25-year life.

## Step 5: Get The Equipment And Paperwork Right

- **Panels:** mono PERC is the current standard, ALMM-listed and BIS-compliant, with ~25-year performance warranties and ~0.5%/year degradation.
- **Inverter:** size to your system; string inverters suit most shops and offices, with hybrid inverters if you plan to add batteries.
- **Net metering:** apply through your DISCOM. Fees are modest - typically Rs 3,000-8,000, up to ~Rs 15,000 for three-phase connections.
- **GST invoice:** insist on a proper invoice with your GSTIN to claim input credit.
- **Sanctioned load:** confirm your connection can support the system, especially if you go beyond 10 kW.

## Do You Need A Battery?

Most shops, clinics and offices on reliable grids do **not** need a battery, because their consumption coincides with generation and net metering handles surplus. A battery adds meaningful cost and is usually justified only where power cuts are frequent and business-critical - for example, a clinic that must keep refrigeration or equipment running through outages. For pure bill savings, skip the battery and size for daytime self-consumption instead.

## Financing Options

You don't have to pay upfront:

- **CAPEX with a business loan or EMI:** own the asset, claim depreciation and GST credit.
- **OPEX / RESCO:** zero upfront cost; buy power from a developer at a lower per-unit rate.

Choose based on your tax position and how long you'll occupy the premises.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What size solar system does a small shop need?**
Often 3-5 kW, generating ~12-20 units/day, enough for lighting, fans and a couple of ACs. Size precisely from your daytime consumption.

**How much does solar cost for a clinic or office?**
In 2026, roughly Rs 2.5-3.5 lakh for 5 kW and Rs 4.5-6.5 lakh for 10 kW, at commercial turnkey rates of ~Rs 38-50/Wp.

**What is the payback for shop or office solar?**
Typically 3-5 years, often at the faster end thanks to high commercial tariffs (Rs 8-12/unit) plus depreciation and GST benefits.

**Do I get the PM Surya Ghar subsidy for my shop?**
No. That central subsidy is residential only. Businesses benefit from accelerated depreciation and GST input credit instead.

**How much roof space do I need?**
About 80-100 sq ft per kW - so ~400-500 sq ft for 5 kW and ~800-1,000 sq ft for 10 kW.

**Do I need a battery for my office?**
Usually not, if your grid is reliable. Batteries mainly help where outages threaten critical equipment, such as a clinic's refrigeration.
