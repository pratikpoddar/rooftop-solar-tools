The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is India's flagship rooftop solar scheme, launched in February 2024 with a target of powering **1 crore homes**. It combines an upfront capital subsidy with the promise of up to **300 free units of electricity per month** for eligible households. If you are an Indian homeowner considering rooftop solar in 2026, this scheme is central to your decision. Here is a complete, plain-language guide.

## What Is the PM Surya Ghar Scheme?

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is a central government subsidy programme that reduces the upfront cost of residential rooftop solar and helps eligible families cut or eliminate their electricity bills. Administered through the official portal **pmsuryaghar.gov.in**, it links homeowners, MNRE-empanelled vendors, and DISCOMs in one workflow, and pays the subsidy directly to your bank account after your system is commissioned.

The scheme targets **residential** consumers. Housing societies and group housing can access it under specific provisions, but the core benefit is designed for individual homes.

## How Much Subsidy Do You Get?

The central subsidy is structured in slabs, based on system capacity:

- **1 kW system:** Rs 30,000
- **2 kW system:** Rs 60,000
- **3 kW and above:** Rs 78,000 (capped)

The underlying slab logic is:

- **Rs 30,000 per kW** for the first 2 kW
- **Rs 18,000 per kW** for the 2-3 kW band
- **Nil** for anything above 3 kW

So the maximum central subsidy any home can receive is **Rs 78,000**, reached at 3 kW. Installing 5 kW or 10 kW is allowed and often sensible, but you receive no additional central subsidy beyond that Rs 78,000 cap.

Importantly, some **states add their own top-up subsidies** on top of the central amount, so your net benefit can be higher depending on where you live.

## The "Up to 300 Free Units" Explained

The scheme's headline promise is up to **300 free units per month** for eligible households. This works through generation plus net metering, not a cash handout: a rightly-sized system (a 3 kW system generates roughly 330-420 units per month, at about 4 units/day per kW) can produce enough to cover a typical household's consumption. Combined with net-metering credits for surplus power exported to the grid, many families see their bills fall to near zero.

In short: the "free electricity" comes from generating your own power and offsetting your grid bill, which the subsidy makes affordable to set up.

## Who Is Eligible?

To qualify for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, you generally need:

- To be an **Indian resident** and owner of the house/roof (or have authorisation).
- A **valid electricity connection** in your name.
- A roof suitable for solar, structurally sound and with adequate sunlight.
- To **not have already availed** a rooftop solar subsidy for the same premises.
- To use an **MNRE-empanelled vendor** and **ALMM-listed modules**, this is mandatory to claim the subsidy.

The subsidy applies to the residential/domestic consumer category. Commercial and industrial connections are not covered by this residential subsidy (though businesses have other benefits like accelerated depreciation).

## How the Money Reaches You: Direct Benefit Transfer

A defining feature of the scheme is transparency. The subsidy is paid via **Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)** straight to your bank account, typically **30-45 days after your DISCOM inspects and commissions the system**. You pay the full system cost (or finance it) upfront, and the government reimburses the subsidy amount afterwards.

This timing matters for cash flow: budget for the full amount initially and treat the subsidy as a reimbursement that arrives about a month or so after commissioning.

## What the Scheme Requires From Your Installation

To keep your subsidy valid, your system must meet compliance standards:

- **ALMM-listed modules:** Panels must be on the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers.
- **MNRE-empanelled vendor:** Your installer must be registered.
- **BIS-compliant components** and proper safety standards.
- **DISCOM inspection and net-meter installation** before commissioning.

Standard residential panels are **mono PERC** modules with ~25-year performance warranties and about 0.5% annual degradation, which comfortably meet the scheme's quality expectations.

## A Worked Example

A family installs a 3 kW system:

- **Pre-subsidy cost:** ~Rs 1.9 lakh
- **Central subsidy:** Rs 78,000 (via DBT after commissioning)
- **Net cost:** ~Rs 1.12 lakh (before any state top-up)
- **Monthly generation:** ~350 units, offsetting most or all of the bill

With a payback period typically in the 3-5 year range, and the system running for ~25 years, this is one of the most cost-effective home upgrades available in India today.

## Concerns and Caveats

- The subsidy is **capped at 3 kW** of benefit; oversizing does not increase the grant.
- You must **fund the cost upfront**; the DBT arrives later.
- Non-compliant components can **void the subsidy**.
- **Net-metering/DISCOM fees** (Rs 3,000-8,000, up to ~Rs 15,000 three-phase) are separate.
- Rules and state top-ups evolve; always confirm current figures on the official portal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much subsidy can I get under PM Surya Ghar?**
Rs 30,000 for 1 kW, Rs 60,000 for 2 kW, and Rs 78,000 for 3 kW and above. The Rs 78,000 is a hard cap; larger systems get no extra central subsidy.

**Is the electricity really free?**
Up to 300 units/month can effectively be free for eligible households, achieved by generating your own power and using net metering to offset your grid bill, not as a cash payout.

**When will I receive the subsidy?**
Via Direct Benefit Transfer to your bank account, typically 30-45 days after your DISCOM inspects and commissions the system.

**Do I have to use a specific installer?**
Yes. You must use an MNRE-empanelled vendor and ALMM-listed modules to qualify. Non-compliant setups lose the subsidy.

**Can businesses use this scheme?**
No, it is for residential consumers. Businesses instead benefit from accelerated depreciation and GST advantages.

**Where do I apply?**
Through the official national portal, pmsuryaghar.gov.in, which links you to your DISCOM and registered vendors.
